# gaea-plugin-ltx

Lightricks' **LTX 2.5** distilled as a [Gaea](https://github.com/xocialize-code/gaea) plugin: video with sound, on MES
Video 0.1's `video.generate`, `video.extend` and `video.retake`. It is built on
[GaeaPluginKit](https://github.com/xocialize-code/gaea-plugin-kit) over
[ltx-2-mlx-swift](https://github.com/xocialize/ltx-2-mlx-swift) (`MLXLTX25Package`), runs in its own process, and speaks
the Gaea Plugin Protocol v1. Add it to Gaea by its repo URL:

```bash
gaea plugins add https://github.com/xocialize-code/gaea-plugin-ltx
```

## The licence

LTX 2.5's weights are under the **LTX-2 Community License**. Its use restrictions bind everyone who uses the model,
an entity with US$10 million or more in annual revenue needs a commercial licence from Lightricks, and what it makes
must not be presented without saying it is machine-generated.
- **The operator accepts it** by enabling each tier: the offers say `accepted_required`, so Gaea lists them off until
  `gaea models enable ltx-2.5-<tier>` (or the switch in Gaea's models list).
- **Each user accepts it** through their client: the offers say `user_accept_required`, so a job runs only when its
  `policy.accepted` lists `LicenseRef-LTX-2-Community`. Otherwise it is 422 `policy_unmet` with reason
  `terms_not_accepted`.
- **Disclosure.** Gaea writes `AI-generated by LTX 2.5 (<tier>)` into every clip's comment item, or
  `AI-generated in part by …` after a source's own comment for extend and retake (Video §1.9).

## What it offers

One model per tier, each with one lane, so a client sees what each tier can do (VIDEO-EVALUATION §6.1):

| Model | `quality` | Size (from a first frame or a clip) | Frames | Stages | DiT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ltx-2.5-compact24` | `draft` | 704×512 (512×288) | 121 | one | int8, streamed; int8 text encoder |
| `ltx-2.5-balanced32` | `draft`, `standard` | 576×320 | 161 | one | int8, streamed; int8 text encoder |
| `ltx-2.5-standard64` | `standard`, `best` | 1280×704 | 161 | two | int8, streamed |
| `ltx-2.5-max128` | `best` | 1920×1088 | 481 | two | bf16, streamed |

With no model named, a request runs on the first tier, in that order, whose gates and limits admit it and that
lists its `quality`. A request for a last frame, say, goes to standard64.

- **`video.generate`** makes a clip with 48 kHz stereo sound from a prompt.
  - **Every tier** takes a first frame.
  - **Two-stage tiers** also take a last frame, up to 5 keyframes at times (`keyframe_times`), and a `soundtrack`.
    The clip follows the soundtrack, which is its sound, unchanged; without `duration_s` the clip is the
    soundtrack's length.
  - **Defaults:** LTX's own, 704×512 (within the tier), 9 frames at 24 fps.
- **`video.extend`** adds `after_s` (default 1 s) after a clip, in whole 8-frame steps. The model reworks the last
  0.5 s of the source so the join is seamless (`overlap_s`).
- **`video.retake`** makes a span of a clip again: `picture`, `sound` or `both` (the default when the clip has sound).
  The span widens to the model's latent frames (frame 0, then eight at a time), with `span_snapped`.
- **Grids.** LTX makes 8k + 1 frames (`frame_step`), on a 32-pixel grid (64 on two-stage tiers, `size_step`).
  - **Generating,** the plugin snaps a request to the nearest value within the tier and warns `duration_snapped`,
    `size_snapped` or `keyframe_moved`. A value past the tier is 422 `out_of_limits`.
  - **Editing** keeps the clip's size and frames, so a clip off the grid (24 frames, or 120 pixels wide) is 422
    `input_invalid`.
- **Seeds.** max128 (bf16) gives identical frames for a seed (`deterministic: true`); the int8 tiers are
  reproducible on one Mac but declare `false`. A job without a seed gets one, reported in `effective.seed`.
- **`audio: false`** gives a silent clip: Gaea leaves the track out.
- **Not yet:** LoRAs and IC-LoRAs (they wait on typed adapters in MLXToolKit, AB-A-0197), extending before a clip,
  and prompt enhancement (`prompt_enhance: on` warns `prompt_enhance_ignored`).

## Weights and memory

The tiers share LTX 2.5's components and its granule tree, which the DiT streams from:

| Repo | Size | Tiers |
|---|---|---|
| `mlx-community/ltx-2.5-mlx` | 67 GB | standard64, max128 (bf16 encoder and VAEs) |
| `mlx-community/ltx-2.5-mlx-q8` | 24.6 GB | compact24, balanced32 (int8 encoder, lean decoder) |
| `xocialize/ltx-2.5-granules` | `q8/` and `bf16/` | every tier |

The engine fetches what a tier's prepare finds missing into Gaea's models root. The package's measured peaks
(AB-R-0106) are about 15 GB on compact24, 17 GB on balanced32, 19–34 GB on standard64, and 74 GB on max128 at
1080p × 481 frames. max128's bf16 lane needs a volume that reads at least 1 GB/s.

## Build and check locally

```bash
scripts/build-plugin.sh
```

```bash
scripts/check-plugin.sh
```

`check-plugin.sh` generates a 256×256, 9-frame clip on max128 over GPP on the signed bundle, with every `.build`
folder hidden. It then shows that the bare worker, outside the bundle, finds no metallib. Its weights come from
`CHECK_MODELS_ROOT` (DEV_VOL1's models folder by default).

`scripts/release.sh` builds, checks, notarizes with the fleet's `mlx-notary` profile, and publishes the GitHub release.
The release workflow does the same in CI when a `v<version>` tag is pushed.

`ltx-inproc` (its header lists the options) is not part of the plugin. It runs a tier in-process through
MLXServeEngine, with the request the worker builds: the reference Gaea's runs are compared against.

```bash
scripts/golden.sh <out-dir>
```

`golden.sh` runs each case over GPP on the signed bundle and through `ltx-inproc` at seed 7. It needs each pair to
decode to the same frames, byte for byte (ffmpeg's `framemd5`), at the reported size and frame count, with 48 kHz
stereo sound. The cases:
- a 256×256, 9-frame clip on each tier;
- a first frame on compact24, and a first and last frame on standard64;
- extend and retake of `Tests/Fixtures/clip.mp4` (128×96, 17 frames, with sound) on compact24.

The kite frames in `Tests/Fixtures` were made by LTX 2.5 itself.

`DEVELOPER_ID_P12_BASE64`, `DEVELOPER_ID_P12_PASSWORD`, `NOTARY_APPLE_ID` and `NOTARY_APP_PASSWORD` (Gaea's
`Docs/Signing secrets.md`).
